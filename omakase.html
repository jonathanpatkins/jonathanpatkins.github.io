Chef Atkins
Atkins was born in San José, where the hum of innovation met the warmth of tradition. A graduate of UC Berkeley, he carries the quiet rigor of academia into the sensual art of dining. His philosophy is simple yet maddeningly elusive: every plate should taste like a memory you can’t quite place.
